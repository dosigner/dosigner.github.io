Atmospheric turbulence vs. beam control.

A conceptual sketch of the FSOC problem: keeping a laser locked on a low-Earth-orbit satellite through a turbulent atmosphere.

Raise the turbulence, then turn beam control on. Control does not remove the turbulence. It pulls the beam back toward the target in that same turbulence.

    Field trials cannot replay the same turbulence on demand, so link decisions leaned on intuition. I wrote uplink and downlink wave-optics simulations, mounted a gimbal terminal emulator on a hexacopter to reproduce tracking motion outdoors, and handed beam pointing to an LSTM-SAC policy.

ABOUT

I don't chase domains. I chase bottlenecks.

I bridge sim-to-real gaps—adapting SOTA robotics models and control loops to real-world hardware and customer needs for rapid deployment.
